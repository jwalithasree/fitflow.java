Activity-Adjusted Water Equation

Target (ml) = Body Weight (kg) × 35 ml × ActivityMultiplier
  • Sedentary: × 1.0 (baseline cellular hydration)
  • Lightly Active: × 1.1 (+10% metabolic fluid exchange)
  • Moderately Active: × 1.2 (+20% sweat & recovery replenishment)
  • Very Active: × 1.35 (+35% intense athletic fluid loss)
